FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Ops Arrow?

Ops Arrow is a retail operations platform for convenience stores. It replaces paper records with digital scratch card control, temperature logs, refusal evidence, compliance checks, safe-drop cash control, store-sales till reports, staff rota & timesheets and visitor sign-in — all under one company account that can manage many shops.

Do my staff and managers use the same thing?

Staff use the mobile app for day-to-day tasks on the shop floor. Owners and managers also get a web portal in the browser for multi-shop oversight, approvals, configuration and reporting.

Can I manage more than one shop?

Yes. One company account can run any number of shops, each with its own subscription. The owner dashboard rolls everything up so you can compare shops and spot what needs attention.

Can I rota staff who don't use the app?

Yes. You can add external / casual staff to the rota and record their hours manually — they flow into your timesheets alongside everyone else. You can also invite them to become full app users at any time.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — every shop starts with a 30-day free trial, no credit card required. Get in touch and we'll get you set up in minutes.

Is my data secure?

Access is role-based — staff, managers and owners each see only what they should — and every plan keeps a clear, timestamped record of activity. Higher plans add an audit log and approval workflows.
